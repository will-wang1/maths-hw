\section{}

Let \( PC = x \). We aim to find the value of \( x \).

\[
AB^2 = PA^2 + PB^2 - 2 \cdot PA \cdot PB \cdot \cos(120^\circ)
\]
\[
AB^2 = 10^2 + 6^2 - 2 \cdot 10 \cdot 6 \cdot (-\tfrac{1}{2}) = 100 + 36 + 60 = 196
\Rightarrow AB = \sqrt{196} = 14
\]
\[
BC^2 = PB^2 + PC^2 - 2 \cdot PB \cdot PC \cdot \cos(120^\circ)
\]
\[
BC^2 = 6^2 + x^2 + 6x = 36 + x^2 + 6x
\]

\[
AC^2 = PA^2 + PC^2 - 2 \cdot PA \cdot PC \cdot \cos(120^\circ)
\]
\[
AC^2 = 10^2 + x^2 + 10x = 100 + x^2 + 10x
\]


Since \( \angle B = 90^\circ \), we have:
\[
AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2 = 196 + (36 + x^2 + 6x) = 232 + x^2 + 6x
\]



\[
100 + x^2 + 10x = 232 + x^2 + 6x
\Rightarrow 100 + 10x = 232 + 6x
\Rightarrow 4x = 132
\Rightarrow x = 33
\]

Hence
\[PC = 33\]
