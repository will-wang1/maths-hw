\section{}
Let \(z = a+bi\). Then we have 
\[|z-4+5i| = 2\sqrt{3} \implies |(a-4)+(b+5)i| = 2\sqrt{3}\]
\[\implies \sqrt{(a-4)^2 + (b+5)^2} = 2\sqrt{3} \implies (a-4)^2 + (b+5)^2 = 12\]
Notice that this is the equation of a circle with centre \(4,-5\) and radius of \(2\sqrt{3}\). Therefore, the area of the circle is 
\[r^2\pi = 12\pi\]
\section{}
Since we have the identity \(|w|^2 = w\overline{w}\) and \(|z| = 1\) then
\[
 z\overline{z}=1.
\]
Hence
\[
|z-1|^2 = (z-1)(\overline{z}-1)
= z\overline{z} - z - \overline{z} + 1
= 1 - z - \overline{z} + 1
= 2 - (z+\overline{z})
\]
and
\[
|z+1|^2 = (z+1)(\overline{z}+1)
= z\overline{z} + z + \overline{z} + 1
= 1 + z + \overline{z} + 1
= 2 + (z+\overline{z})
\]
Therefore
\[
|z-1|^2 + |z+1|^2
= \big(2 - (z+\overline{z})\big) + \big(2 + (z+\overline{z})\big)
= 4
\]
